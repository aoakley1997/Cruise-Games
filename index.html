📖 How to play

The aim of the game

Earn points by spotting funny cruise behaviour, finding ridiculous Facebook posts and completing side quests.

👥
1. Choose your player

Each device remembers its own player profile.

📅
2. Select the cruise day

Day 1 is 22 October; Day 9 is 30 October. Port days are Day 4 Port Vila, Day 5 Mystery Island and Day 6 Nouméa.

☀️
3. Daily challenges

Spot the behaviour before claiming the daily challenge. Each player can claim once per day.

⚖️
4. Cruise Courtroom — vote!

Alex, Mum and Dad each cast one private vote: Guilty or Not guilty. Votes stay hidden until all three have voted. The majority option wins and every player who voted for that option earns the courtroom points. If votes tie, there is no winning option and nobody earns courtroom points that day. Each player may vote only once per day.

🎯
5. Passenger bingo

Each player has a different card per day. Tap squares you spot. Complete a row, column or diagonal for a bonus; each line bonus is awarded once per card.

📱
6. Facebook bingo

Each post type can be claimed once by the whole family for the entire cruise.

🧭
7. Side quests

Each quest can be claimed once by one cruiser for the entire game.

🔮
8. Predictions

The creator owns the prediction. Mark it correct or incorrect when settled. You can delete a prediction; deleting a correct one removes its awarded points.

🏆
9. Leaderboard

Scores sync when connected to the internet. Export a backup occasionally.

Be respectful, don't disturb other passengers, and don't photograph or identify strangers for the game. Firebase syncing requires internet access.
