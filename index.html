📖 How to play

Welcome aboard

The aim of the game

Earn the most points by spotting funny cruise behaviour, finding ridiculous Facebook posts and completing side quests. The player with the highest score at the end wins family bragging rights.

👥
1. Choose the player

Tap your player card at the top before claiming points. Each device remembers its own selected player.

📅
2. Select the cruise day

Change the day at the top as the cruise progresses. Daily challenges, courtroom claims, bingo cards and Facebook claims are tracked by day.

☀️
3. Complete daily challenges

Spot the behaviour in real life before claiming the challenge. Each player can claim the daily challenge and courtroom verdict once per day.

⚖️
4. The Cruise Courtroom

Read the day’s ridiculous case and discuss it as a family. Decide on a verdict together, then tap “Deliver verdict” for your player. Each player can deliver one verdict per cruise day and earn the courtroom points once that day.

🎯
5. Play passenger bingo

Each player has a different card for each day. Tap a square when you see the behaviour. Complete a row, column or diagonal to earn a bonus. A line bonus is awarded only once per line on that card.

📱
6. Watch the Facebook group

When someone spots a listed post type, the person who spotted it should claim it using their player profile. Each listed post type can only be claimed once by the whole family for the entire game, so it is unavailable to everyone after the first claim.

🧭
7. Complete side quests

These optional activities can only be claimed once by one cruiser for the entire game. Choose the player who completed the quest; after it is claimed, it is done for everyone.

🔮
8. Make predictions

The person creating a prediction owns it. When the outcome is known, mark it correct or incorrect. Correct predictions award points once.

🏆
9. Check the leaderboard

Scores update when points are earned. Check the leaderboard on your final cruise day.

💡 Customising your game

Change challenges and observations

Open Scores → Settings & customisation. Edit the text boxes and put one item on each line. Daily challenges need a title and description separated by a vertical bar: Title | Description. Save custom lists when finished.

Save your progress

Progress syncs through Firebase when you’re signed in and connected to the internet. Export a backup periodically. You can delete a prediction if it was added by mistake; deleting a correct prediction also removes the points awarded for it.

Avoid disputes

Agree on the rules before starting. Be honest about sightings, don't disturb other passengers, and don't photograph or identify strangers for the game. It's meant to be a silly family competition, not a confrontation.
